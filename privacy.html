WORKOUT PILOT / PRIVACY

Your workout.
Your information.

Workout Pilot stores your workout plans on your device. It does not require an account or send your workout data to a developer-operated server.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who is responsible

Workout Pilot is developed by Tomas Regina. For privacy questions or requests, contact tomasregina1@gmail.com. This policy covers the Workout Pilot iOS app and this support website.

Information stored in the app

The app stores workout names, exercise selections, sets, repetitions, durations, rest settings, favorites, and app preferences locally on your device. This information is used to provide the workout experience. The developer does not receive this information through the app.

The app does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, track you across apps or websites, or access your location, contacts, microphone, or Apple Health data. Voice coaching plays bundled recordings and may use Apple’s system speech for names without a recording; it does not record you or send workout prompts to a developer-operated AI service.

Purchases and Apple services

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit framework to check verified purchase entitlements and unlock Plus features. The developer does not receive your card details. Apple processes purchase and service information under its own privacy policy. Apple may make sales reports and diagnostics available to developers through its services, subject to Apple’s settings and policies.

When you contact support

If you email support, your email address, message, and any attachments are processed to answer your request and resolve the issue. Email is handled through Gmail, subject to Google’s privacy policy. Please only include information needed to explain your request.

Support correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask for deletion using the contact address above.

This website

This site uses no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, contact forms, or developer-set cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even if visitors are not signed into GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its processing and retention practices.

Storage, deletion, and your choices

You can edit or delete saved workouts in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data. Your operating system’s backup and restore services may retain or restore app data according to your device and Apple Account settings. The developer cannot access or delete your device backups. Purchase records are managed by Apple and are not erased by deleting the app.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, request portability, or complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise rights relating to information you have sent to the developer. For information held independently by Apple, Google, or GitHub, use their privacy controls or contact them directly.

How information is used and shared

Support information is used to provide requested help and maintain the app, based on our legitimate interest in assisting users, or to meet legal obligations where applicable. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Service providers process information as described above; information may also be disclosed where required by law. These providers may process information in countries other than your own under their respective privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new revision date. For questions about this policy, email tomasregina1@gmail.com.
