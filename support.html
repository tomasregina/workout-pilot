Common questions

Does Workout Pilot work offline?

Workout planning and bundled English voice coaching work offline. Loading purchase information, buying Plus, and restoring purchases require an internet connection.

Which exercises are free?

Push-ups, squats, lunges, plank, side plank, jumping jacks, glute bridges, calf raises, superman, and bird dog are free. Plus unlocks the full library of 887 exercises.

How do I restore Workout Pilot Plus?

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. In Settings, open the Workout Pilot Plus section and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are connected to the internet. Plus is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Apple handles billing and refund requests. You can request help at Apple’s Report a Problem website.

Why is the coach speaking English?

The interface supports English, Slovak, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Polish, and Hungarian. Voice guidance is currently available in English only. Changing the interface language does not change the coach’s voice.

Where are my workouts saved?

Workouts, favorites, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Workout Pilot does not provide its own cloud sync or account system. Deleting the app removes its local data; device backups may retain a copy depending on your Apple settings.

How do I change the order of exercises?

Open the workout detail, touch and hold an exercise, then drag it to the position you want. Tap Save to keep the workout. Use Edit Multiple to adjust settings for selected exercises on the same screen.

Why does my workout pause when I leave the app?

Workout Pilot pauses when you leave the app or an audio interruption occurs. Return to the workout and resume when you are ready.
